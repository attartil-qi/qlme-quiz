AtTartil · Form I practice — for each ayah, choose the translation that captures the correct tense & doer, then identify the word type (ṣarf).

Every word in this quiz comes from the عبد root, Form I only (فعل ماضي / مضارع / أمر, and its nominal relatives: اسم فاعل, مصدر, اسم). Since the root meaning repeats throughout, the quiz does not re-test vocabulary — instead, each translation option changes the person and/or tense, so picking correctly means you've identified the doer (الفاعل) and tense from the Arabic form itself. Several ayāt also carry an attached pronoun — as a verb's object (e.g. اعْبُدْهُ, "worship Him") or a noun's possessor (e.g. عِبَادِي, "My slaves") — so picking correctly there means translating that pronoun too.
